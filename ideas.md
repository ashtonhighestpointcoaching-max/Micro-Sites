# ideas — invernessroofing.com

- The entire site is driven by `client/src/data/{site,services,towns,faqs}.ts`.
  A second county is a fork with three data files swapped — that is the
  portfolio play, and it is why the data/presentation split is strict.
- Mobile home roofing deserves its own deeper hub: roof-over vs recoat vs
  replace is a genuine decision homeowners search for and nobody local answers.
- Coastal vs inland is a real content axis in this county (Crystal River and
  Homosassa lose fasteners to salt; Beverly Hills is a uniform-age tract).
  Worth a comparison page — good AI-Overview bait, and true.
- Storm season: a dated "after the storm" page refreshed each hurricane is the
  highest-intent traffic in Florida roofing.
